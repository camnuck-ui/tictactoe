Otis Says Hello

A story about feeling shy — and one small wave.
Meet the Fairweathers!
OtisKitGus MomDad & Rudder
Paw-Paw Mom’s mom — and today’s big hello!
2
Where to?

One morning in Maple Cove, Mom spun the globe. “Guess where Bluebell flies today? We’re going to see Paw-Paw!”

Kit cheered. But Otis hugged his little blue plane tight. “I only know Paw-Paw from the computer,” he whispered.

3
It’s okay to go quiet

“Hong Kong” means fragrant harbor!

“What if I go all quiet?” asked Otis.

Dad crouched down beside him. “Then you go quiet, buddy. That’s okay.”

Otis looked at the globe. “But… why is it called Hong Kong?”

“It means fragrant harbor,” said Mom. “Fragrant? Like… smelly-nice?” Otis scrunched his nose.

4
Snug as a bun
Gus

On Bluebell, Gus held up a paper bakery bag. “Tuck your bag under the seat, snug as a bun!”

Click, click went the seatbelts. Gus crouched beside Otis. “My tummy feels wiggly,” Otis whispered.

“New hellos make my tummy wiggle too,” said Gus kindly. “You don’t have to be brave all at once.”

5
Up in the clouds

Above the soft white clouds, the flight was calm and smooth.

“Paw-Paw loves riding the tram up the Peak to watch the black kites,” said Mom.

“I’ll say hi first,” said Kit. “Then you can just wave.” “A wave,” Otis whispered. “I can do a wave.”

Then Dad’s voice crackled over the speaker: “What’s a pilot’s favorite snack? Plane crackers!” Otis giggled.

6
Hello, Paw-Paw!
1a tiny wave

At the Peak Tram station, there she was! Kit hurried into a great big hug.

Otis hid behind Dad’s leg at first. Paw-Paw crouched down, a few steps away, and spoke to his little blue plane.

“Hello, little plane. Do you like trains?”

Otis lifted one hand and gave a tiny wave. Paw-Paw waved back, just as tiny.

7

Thank you, when someone helps you.

The café was so busy! Every little table was full. Paw-Paw smiled at a grandpa at a big round table. “Can we share, please?”

“Please, sit!” he said. “Mm-GOY,” said Paw-Paw.

Kit was giggling with Rudder and missed it. But Otis watched closely. “In a busy café, we share tables,” Paw-Paw whispered. “You just ask nicely.”

8
Pineapple buns

No pineapple in a pineapple bun! The sugar top just looks like pineapple skin.

Then came warm, golden pineapple buns! “Pineapple! My favorite fruit!” said Dad.

He broke his bun open and peered inside. “Wait… where’s the pineapple?”

Paw-Paw laughed. “There’s no pineapple in a pineapple bun! The crunchy sugar top just looks like pineapple skin.”

“Still delicious!” said Dad.

9
Up, up, up!

Up, up, up the steep hill went the little green tram.

“I rode this tram every Saturday,” said Paw-Paw, “when I was Otis’s size.”

“Look!” giggled Kit. “The buildings are leaning!”

“They only look tipped,” Paw-Paw laughed. “The hill is so steep, it tricks your eyes.”

10
Black kites

Black kites ride warm air, so they glide with only a few flaps!

2a whispered question

At the top, the whole harbor sparkled far below. Black kites glided past on still wings. Rudder flapped and flapped.

Otis shuffled closer to Paw-Paw. “But… why don’t they flap?” he whispered.

“Good question!” Paw-Paw whispered back. “The sun warms the hill, and warm air rises. The kites ride it up, like an elevator.”

11
Every table is full!

Dad came back with hot egg waffles. “Kit, Otis, can you find us a place to sit?” asked Paw-Paw.

“Easy!” said Kit. She checked one table. Full! She checked another. Full!

“Every table is full!” Kit’s shoulders drooped. “We’ll have to stand. Or wait forever.”

12
Otis asks
3ask — and say thank you

Otis looked around quietly. He spotted a round table with three empty stools.

“Kit,” he whispered, “we can share. Like Paw-Paw did at breakfast. You just ask nicely.”

Otis took a big breath. “Hello. Can we share, please?”

“Of course! Sit, sit,” said the dad. “Mm-GOY!” said Otis, beaming.

13
A little tram

Paw-Paw’s toy tram from when she was little — now it’s Otis’s!

“I’m good at watching,” Otis said with a shy grin. “The best watcher,” said Kit. “Thanks, Otis!”

Before they flew home, Paw-Paw crouched down. “This was mine when I was little. Now it’s yours.”

A toy Peak Tram! Otis set his little plane on top. Then he hugged Paw-Paw, all on his own. “Bye, Paw-Paw.”
